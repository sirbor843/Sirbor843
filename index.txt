About me

I build data and ML systems that stay fast, reliable, and cheap to run. Over the last few years, I’ve built real-time payment layers, event pipelines, and automated workflows across fintech, gaming, and healthcare—routinely halving latency and cutting cloud bills by over a third. I prefer working directly with users to dig through messy requirements, stress-test assumptions, and turn real-world edge cases into clean, reliable features shipped straight to production.
